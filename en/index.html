About us

The AGH AVADER Student Research Club was founded in 2014/2015 and is supervised by the Embedded Perception and Autonomous Systems Group (Vision Systems Laboratory, Department of Automatic Control and Robotics, AGH University of Krakow, Poland).

We work on embedded perception and control systems for mobile robots: autonomous drones (UAVs), self-driving cars, advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and advanced video surveillance. We use data from video, thermal and event cameras, radar and LiDAR, processed with classical algorithms and embedded AI (TinyML), in ROS 2 and simulators (Gazebo, NVIDIA Isaac Sim), including hardware-in-the-loop tests.

Our hardware includes FPGA and SoC FPGA boards (ZCU104, Kria, Versal), NVIDIA Jetson and Raspberry Pi 5, platform, FPV racing and swarm drones, a 3D printer, a motion-capture cage and an autonomous four-wheeled vehicle.

We compete in international competitions (ICUAS UAV Competition, MathWorks MiniDrone, Xilinx Open Hardware), present at AGH student research conferences, publish papers and take part in AGH Open Days and Researchers’ Night.
